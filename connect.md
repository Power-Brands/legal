# Connect to Powerbrands Custom MCP

The Powerbrands Custom MCP is an internal Powerbrands tool that connects to QuickBooks Online for internal accounting and sales workflows. It is not offered to external customers or third parties.

If you are an authorized Powerbrands employee or contractor and you have been asked to connect or reconnect QuickBooks Online to this tool, please follow the OAuth flow initiated from your terminal or internal admin interface.

If you have arrived at this page unexpectedly, you can safely close the window.
