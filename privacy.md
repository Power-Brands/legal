# Powerbrands Custom MCP — Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** May 13, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how the Powerbrands Custom MCP application ("the Application") collects, uses, and protects information when used by authorized Powerbrands personnel to interact with QuickBooks Online via Intuit's APIs.

## 1. Who This Applies To

The Application is an internal-use tool intended only for authorized employees and contractors of Powerbrands. It is not offered to the general public or third-party customers. This policy applies to data accessed through the Application during the course of authorized internal business operations.

## 2. Information We Access

When authorized Powerbrands personnel use the Application, it may access the following categories of data through Intuit's QuickBooks Online APIs:

- Customer records (names, email addresses, billing and shipping addresses, phone numbers)
- Invoice and estimate data (line items, amounts, dates, payment status, payment links)
- Product and service catalog data
- Account and company configuration data necessary to operate on invoices and customers

The Application does not access banking credentials, employee Social Security Numbers, or payroll detail beyond what authorized Powerbrands personnel already have permission to view in QuickBooks Online.

## 3. How We Use Information

Accessed data is used solely to:

- Create, update, retrieve, and send invoices on behalf of Powerbrands
- Look up and create customer records in support of Powerbrands' sales operations
- Generate and retrieve payment links for invoices issued to Powerbrands' customers
- Support related internal business workflows

We do not sell, rent, or share QuickBooks Online data with any third party.

## 4. How Data Is Handled

- All data exchanged between the Application and Intuit's APIs is transmitted over HTTPS using OAuth 2.0 authorization.
- OAuth refresh tokens are stored encrypted and accessible only to the Application's runtime.
- The Application does not maintain a persistent copy of customer data outside of what is required for an active operation. Data fetched from QuickBooks Online is held only for the duration needed to complete the requested task and is not retained in long-term storage by the Application.
- Logs may retain operational metadata (e.g., invoice IDs, timestamps, user identifiers) for debugging and audit purposes. These logs do not include full customer payment details.

## 5. Data Retention

The Application itself does not act as a system of record. The system of record remains QuickBooks Online, governed by Intuit's own retention practices and Powerbrands' QuickBooks Online configuration. Operational logs maintained by the Application are retained no longer than reasonably necessary for debugging and audit, generally not exceeding 90 days.

## 6. Security

We implement reasonable technical and organizational measures designed to protect data accessed by the Application. No system is fully immune to risk; if you suspect a security incident, please contact us immediately at the address below.

## 7. Third-Party Services

The Application accesses data through Intuit's QuickBooks Online APIs. Intuit's handling of that data is governed by Intuit's own privacy policy, available at https://www.intuit.com/privacy/.

## 8. Your Rights

Because the Application is an internal Powerbrands tool, individuals whose data appears in QuickBooks Online (for example, Powerbrands' customers) should direct privacy requests to Powerbrands' standard customer-facing privacy channels rather than to this Application. Authorized Powerbrands users can revoke the Application's access to QuickBooks Online at any time through Intuit's connected-apps settings.

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The latest version will be posted at this URL with an updated effective date.
